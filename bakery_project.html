Data Exploration Summary:
The dataset contains transactional bakery sales data with features such as date, item name, quantity sold, price, daypart, and day type. A review of the dataset shows no missing values across all columns. Summary statistics indicate stable pricing and a wide range of sales volumes across products.

Data Cleaning:
The dataset was checked for missing and duplicate values. No null values were found, and no duplicate records required removal. Therefore, no additional data cleaning was necessary.

Conclusion¶

This analysis examined bakery sales data to identify top-selling products, key revenue drivers, and time-based trends. Results show that a small number of products generate the majority of sales and revenue, with demand peaking during specific time periods and weekends. These insights can support data-driven decisions related to inventory planning, staffing, and promotional strategies.
